# ✈️ Marketing & Innovation Strategy Plan for Australian Tour

## 📌 Executive Summary & Company Profile
The **Australian Tour** story began in the mid-2000s (specifically 2010) as a group of college students trying to pitch their travel agency idea on the television show **"Shark Tank"**. Their core concept was making travel across Australia affordable and accessible for millennials. After successfully pitching their start-up to the judges, they secured approval and funding. 

Today, Australian Tour is recognized as one of the premier and most recommendable travel agencies in Australia, with operations spanning across all six Australian states.

### 🎓 The Founders
Australian Tour was founded by Swinburne University of Technology (SUT) student **Zayed Al Kathiri**, alongside co-founders **Angus Bergman**, **Julian Kozulin**, and **Henry Starkins**. Together, these four college students built a successful national travel enterprise.

### 🎯 Mission Statement
*“To help as many millennials as we can to travel to Australia, by doing it one at a time.”*

## 🌟 STAR Method Summary (Portfolio Highlight)

- **📌 Situation:** Australian Tour required a strategic scaling and modernization plan to bridge traditional high-touch physical store experiences with a digital-first mobile booking channel for millennials and eco-demographic travelers.
- **🎯 Task:** Formalize the company’s vision, mission, and growth objectives into an actionable innovation roadmap, culminating in a $300,000 AUD mobile app rollout.
- **⚙️ Action:** 
  * Structured the company's dual-channel model (eco-friendly physical stores in city centers combined with digital web/app booking).
  * Defined rigorous SMART targets for brand authority and technological expansion.
  * Designed a mobile app interface wireframe highlighting streamlined travel links and booking functionalities.
- **📈 Impact:** Established a clear strategic framework that balances traditional customer service excellence with millennial digital engagement, supporting long-term national expansion across all six Australian states.

## 🏢 Business Model & Operations

### 🏬 Our Stores & Physical Presence
* **Locations:** Operating across six different states in Australia.
* **Store Environment:** Situated in safe, eco-friendly city-center locations welcoming walk-in customers.
* **Target Audience Expansion:** Beyond millennials, the physical stores target **eco-demographic segments**, proving the brand caters to diverse macro-segments rather than a single age group.
* **Traditional & In-Store Strengths:** Highly skilled staff assisting customers with activities, bookings, and local food delivery. Traditional visual merchandising showcases detailed hotel room layouts and destination content.
* **Digital Integration:** Fully integrated websites and digital booking pathways tailored for younger demographics who prefer booking online.

## 🔭 Vision Statement Framework

> *"A vision statement is an anchor point of any strategy plan. It outlines what an organization would like to ultimately achieve and gives purpose to the existence of the organization. A vision statement should be simple, short, and specific to your business and leave nothing open to interpretation"* (Wright 2019). 

* **Australian Tour Vision Statement:** 
  > **"To create an opportunity for everyone who enjoys travelling around the world"**
* **Strategic Intent:** The vision highlights the agency's commitment to supporting budget-conscious travelers and individuals who have a passion for global and domestic exploration.

---

## 📈 SMART Objectives Framework

> *"An objective is a statement which describes what an individual, team or organization is hoping to achieve. Objectives are 'SMART' if they are specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, and timely bound"* (Chartered Management Institute 2011).

* **Australian Tour Core SMART Objective:** 
  * **By December 2022**, the Australian Tour Company will be recognized as the **most recommendable travel agency company** for travel in Australia.
  * **Within the next 2 years**, the company will launch its own **proprietary mobile application** featuring an extensive directory of vital travel information and seamless booking links, representing a total investment budget of **$300,000 AUD**.
